The honest answer to "how much is nanoplasty?" is "it depends on your hair," and that's frustrating when you're trying to budget. So here are real ranges, what moves the price, and how to compare it with other treatments.

## Typical price range

In the Chicago area, nanoplasty typically runs about **$350–$550** per session.

| Hair | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Short / chin-length | Low end of the range |
| Shoulder-length, medium density | Middle of the range |
| Long or very thick | Upper end, sometimes higher |

These are typical guide ranges, not quotes. Prices change, and every salon sets its own. Always confirm the **total** price for your hair before booking.

## What drives the price

1. **Length and density.** More hair means more product and much more ironing time. This is the biggest factor.
2. **Product brand.** Premium nanoplasty lines cost salons more per ounce.
3. **Stylist experience.** Specialists who do nanoplasty all the time often charge more, and are usually worth it.
4. **Location.** Downtown salons tend to charge more than neighborhood and suburban specialists. See our [Northwest Side guide](/blog/nanoplasty-northwest-side-chicago/).
5. **Hair condition.** Bleached or fragile hair may need a strand test, a gentler process, or a repair treatment first.

## Hidden costs to ask about

- **Aftercare shampoo.** Sulfate-free and salt-free shampoo is required. Some salons require a specific brand, often $25–$60 for shampoo and conditioner.
- **Trim.** Some salons include it, and others charge separately.
- **Tip.** For a 3–4 hour service, tips are typically based on the full price.
- **Touch-ups.** Ask whether any adjustment within the first couple of weeks is included.

## Cost per month: the comparison that matters

A cheaper treatment isn't always cheaper over a year. Here's a rough example using mid-range numbers:

| Treatment | Example price | Lasts about | Cost per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nanoplasty | $450 | 4–5 months | ~$90–$115 |
| Traditional keratin | $375 | 3–4 months | ~$95–$125 |
| Express keratin | $200 | 6–8 weeks | ~$100–$135 |
| Cysteine | $250 | 2–3 months | ~$85–$125 |

Real numbers depend on your hair and aftercare, but the pattern holds: **longer-lasting treatments often close the price gap.** Good aftercare stretches the per-month cost further. See [how long nanoplasty lasts](/blog/how-long-does-nanoplasty-last/).

## Ways to keep the cost down

- **Book on a weekday.** Some salons price off-peak appointments lower.
- **Stretch the time between sessions** with sulfate-free shampoo, a shower filter, and fewer chlorine swims.
- **Treat only where you need it.** If your ends are fine and your roots frizz, ask about a regrowth-only touch-up for your next appointment.
- **Don't shop on price alone.** A badly done nanoplasty that needs fixing costs more than a good one done once.
