Nanoplasty typically lasts **3 to 6 months**. That's a wide range, and where you land in it depends mostly on two things you control: what you wash with and how often.

## Why the range is so wide

| Factor | Pushes toward 3 months | Pushes toward 6 months |
|---|---|---|
| **Shampoo** | Regular shampoo with sulfates or salt | Sulfate-free, salt-free |
| **Wash frequency** | Daily | 2–3 times a week |
| **Water** | Hard water, no filter | Shower filter, occasional clarifying |
| **Swimming** | Frequent chlorine or salt water | Rarely, or rinsed right away |
| **Heat styling** | High heat every day | Air-dry or low heat |
| **Hair type** | Very curly or coarse (texture returns sooner) | Wavy or frizzy-straight |
| **Application** | Rushed ironing | Thorough, small-section ironing |

## How it fades

Nanoplasty doesn't stop working all at once. It wears off gradually with each wash, and your natural texture slowly returns. Because it isn't a permanent relaxer, there's no hard line where new growth meets treated hair. Most people notice frizz creeping back at the roots and crown first, then in humid weather.

## Aftercare that makes it last longer

### The big one: your shampoo

Use **sulfate-free and salt-free** shampoo (check for "sodium chloride" on the label). Sulfates and salt are the fastest way to strip a smoothing treatment. This one change makes more difference than everything else on this list combined.

### Wash less often

Every wash removes a little of the treatment. If you wash daily now, try every other day, using dry shampoo in between if needed.

### Handle Chicago water

Lake Michigan tap water is moderately hard, and minerals build up on smoothed hair. A **shower filter** helps, and so does an occasional sulfate-free clarifying wash if your stylist approves it. More in [why Chicago hair needs nanoplasty](/blog/nanoplasty-for-chicago-hair/).

### Protect it in the pool and at the lake

Wet your hair with clean water and apply a leave-in before swimming, then rinse right after.

### Go easy on heat

One of nanoplasty's benefits is that you need less heat styling. When you do use heat, use a heat protectant and moderate temperatures.

### Condition, but lightly

Use a conditioner or mask that's compatible with smoothing treatments, mostly from mid-lengths to ends. Heavy oils at the roots can weigh smoothed hair down.

## The first few days matter

Follow your stylist's instructions exactly for the first 48–72 hours, since some products need time to set. Washing rules vary by product. See [what to expect at your appointment](/blog/nanoplasty-appointment-what-to-expect/).

## When to book your next session

Most people rebook when:

- frizz is back on humid days,
- styling time is creeping back up, or
- the roots behave noticeably differently from the ends.

For many Chicago clients that works out to two sessions a year: late spring before humidity, and fall before the dry indoor-heat months. Waiting until your hair feels fully back to normal is better than redoing it too soon, because repeat treatments too close together can stress the hair.

| Treatment | Typically lasts |
|---|---|
| Nanoplasty | 3–6 months |

| Brazilian blowout | ~3 months |
| Cysteine | 6 weeks–3 months |
