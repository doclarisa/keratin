## Finding a real nanoplasty specialist

A lot of salons list "smoothing treatments" without saying which one. To check that a salon actually does nanoplasty:

1. **Look for the word "nanoplasty" or "nanoplastia"** on their service menu, not just "keratin."
2. **Check their social media** for nanoplasty results on real clients with hair like yours.
3. **Call and ask which product they use.** Experienced stylists answer right away.
4. **Ask how often they do it.** Several a month is a good sign. "We can do that" is not.

Many nanoplasty specialists on the Northwest Side and in the west suburbs serve Spanish-, Portuguese-, and Polish-speaking clients. Nanoplasty first became popular in Brazil, and stylists trained in Brazilian smoothing techniques often have the most experience with it. If a language other than English is easier for you, ask; many salons in the area are bilingual.

- **Time:** block out half a day. Weekday mornings are often easiest to book.

- **Kids or errands:** you'll be in the chair a long time, so arrange pickups ahead.
